In Arcade, you explore a turn-based maze, collect loot, and work toward the final encounter on floor 10.

Use the **arrow keys** to move and attack. Moving spends Energy, while ordinary attacks cost none. Your run ends if your Energy reaches zero.

Watch enemy attack patterns as you explore. Learning when to move out of danger helps you preserve Energy for the floors ahead.

Find **stairs** to progress. The maze changes between runs, so explore your current floor rather than relying on a fixed route.

Collect XP to level up and choose talents when offered. These choices shape your abilities for the rest of the run.

After defeating **Sir Jackalot**, hit the large reward chest repeatedly to open it, collect your loot, then use the unlocked exit to finish the run.
