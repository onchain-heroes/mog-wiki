**Energy keeps your run going.** If it reaches zero, the run ends.

You start with **100 base Energy**. A normal dungeon move costs **1 Energy**, while ordinary attacks cost none. Plan your route with that movement cost in mind.

Effects can change the usual rules. For example, frostbite makes attacks cost Energy while it is active. Check the [weather effects](/reference/hazards/) shown in your interface.

Leveling raises your base Energy. A [Shrine](/reference/encounters/#shrine) also lets you spend Treasure to restore Energy; review the displayed recovery and price before confirming an offering.

## Free movement

Movement is free in the Shrine, Armory and Sanctum, and after you defeat the final boss. Moving during the boss fight still costs Energy.
