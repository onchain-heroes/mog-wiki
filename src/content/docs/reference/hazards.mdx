Open the **weather indicator** to read the effects currently affecting your floor. Biome weather lasts until you leave the floor; common weather is temporary.

## Miasma

Enemy attacks apply **poison**, which deals damage over time. Another hit refreshes the effect.

<TurnDemo kind="miasma" />

## Blizzard

Drops can freeze, preventing you from revealing or collecting them. **Bump a frozen drop once** to crack its ice. Waiting does not thaw it.

Enemy attacks can also apply **frostbite**, making your attacks cost Energy while the effect lasts. Another hit refreshes frostbite.

<TurnDemo kind="ice" />

## Heatwave

**Mirage** makes attack charge timing less predictable for many enemies. Their attack cue is still shown.

Enemy attacks also apply **burn**, which deals damage over time.

<TurnDemo kind="heatwave" />

## Gale

Gusts push you, enemies, and loose drops one tile downwind. Rooted enemies stay in place.

<TurnDemo kind="gale" />

## Storm

Lightning marks a tile before striking on the following turn. **Move off the mark before it lands.** Lightning can damage both you and enemies.

<TurnDemo kind="lightning" />

## Floor traps

[Spikes](/reference/cells/#spikes), [wall-arrow traps](/reference/cells/#wall-arrow-traps) and [lava](/reference/cells/#lava) are dangers separate from weather. Read their entries before crossing. Attacking or waiting still advances a turn; a spike being down now does not guarantee it will stay down after your next action.
