An Arcade run leads through **10 floors**. The floor banner shows your progress and the current area.

| Floors | Area           |
| ------ | -------------- |
| 1–2    | Trial Caves    |
| 3–4    | Poison Marsh   |
| 5–7    | Frostfields    |
| 8–9    | Gemglow Depths |
| 10     | Sir Jackalot   |

Find stairs to continue your descent. Along the way, [special rooms](/reference/encounters/) offer opportunities to recover Energy or buy items.

**The Sanctum** is the last preparation stop before the boss arena. Finish your preparations before descending; you cannot return once you enter the arena.

These area names describe **MoG Arcade**. This page does not map other game modes or editions.

## Enemies by floor

These lists include enemies that can appear directly or through summons, plus mimics. They describe possible encounters, not a guarantee that every listed enemy appears in each run. Select an enemy for its behavior and counterplay.

## Read the environment

[Weather](/reference/hazards/) changes how a floor plays. Check its indicator when you arrive. [Special tiles](/reference/cells/) explain fountains, spikes, wall arrows, portals and pickups. Molten lava centres belong to the late floors; the other coloured floor patterns are not automatically contact hazards.
