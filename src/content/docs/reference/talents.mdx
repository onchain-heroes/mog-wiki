## Talent catalogue

These are the 29 talents available in this edition of Arcade. The descriptions explain their effects and trade-offs; read the current choice card for its displayed strength and enhancement tier.

<Catalogue kind="talents" />

## Leveling up

Collect **XP** to level up, up to **level 15**. Each level raises your base Energy.

Your offensive stats improve as you level:

- **Levels 1–10:** ATK increases.
- **Levels 11–15:** critical-hit chance increases.

## Choosing talents

Selected levels offer **talents**. They range from damage increases to effects that change how you play. Read the offered descriptions before choosing.

Once you have selected **five talents**, you are offered **enhancements** instead.

The frame shows a talent’s rarity. Enhancements add stars without changing that rarity.
