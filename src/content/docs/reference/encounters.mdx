## Shrine

<RoomScreenshot room="shrine" />

Attack the shrine once to inspect it, then attack again to make an offering. Spend **Treasure** to restore Energy. Review the recovery amount and price shown before that second interaction.

You can make further offerings while you can afford them, but each offering costs more than the last. Leave by the stairs when you are ready to continue.

## Armory

<RoomScreenshot room="armory" />

Inspect the items on the pedestals and confirm the purchase you want. Check the displayed price and your available Treasure before buying.

If your inventory is full, use or discard an item before buying. The shop requires an empty slot.

When you have finished shopping, take the stairs to continue.

See the [item catalogue](/reference/equipment/) for effects, charges and restrictions.

## The Sanctum

<RoomScreenshot room="sanctum" />

The Sanctum is your **last preparation stop before the boss arena**. Make your final preparations here before descending.

There is no return to the Sanctum once you enter the arena.

## Unknown Trainer

<RoomScreenshot room="unknown-trainer" />

A trainer can appear during ordinary exploration. Bump the trainer and review the Energy cost. Choose an eligible owned talent to exchange for a **random new talent**; the replacement is revealed after the swap. Enhancements on the old talent are lost.
