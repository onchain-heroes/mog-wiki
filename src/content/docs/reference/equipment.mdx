These are the 15 obtainable items in this edition of Arcade. Items give you a limited-use action; check the item icon and targeting prompt before activating one.

Collect a dropped item by stepping onto its tile. If your inventory is full, the game offers a choice to keep your items or replace one. Replacing discards the selected old item.

You can also buy items in the [Armory](/reference/encounters/), which requires an empty inventory slot. Use or discard an item to make room before buying.

Some items have more than one charge. Relocation tools have room restrictions, and several control effects do not work on the final boss. Each entry explains the relevant limits.
