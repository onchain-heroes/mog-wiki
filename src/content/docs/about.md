This field guide covers **MoG V2 Arcade on Abstract**, in English, French, Spanish, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese. It is a reference for the things you see and the decisions you make during a run.

## What is covered

- Movement, Energy, leveling and all 29 available talents.
- All 10 floors, their possible enemy rosters and the final boss.
- A bestiary of 22 enemies, including summoned creatures and mimics.
- 27 types of terrain, hazards, interactive objects and pickups.
- The 15 usable items, weather effects and reachable special rooms.

The bestiary uses descriptive labels where creatures have no displayed name. Translated entries also show the English label so players can find the same entry across languages.

## Game recordings and screenshots

Videos and room screenshots are recorded locally with the original game renderer and animations. The demonstrations use controlled situations to make each mechanic easy to follow. Shop stock and prices shown are examples. Press play to watch a recording, or download its GIF. Written explanations are available in every supported language.

## Keeping it useful

This edition was checked against the game's guide, interface, current source behavior and the recorded local scenarios. It has not yet been verified through a complete live playthrough. Each page shows its review date. Updates can change the details, so check the current in-game prompt when making a decision.

Language coverage is checked automatically. Translations should still receive a fluent-speaker review before publication.

Expedition and other variants are outside this edition. Event-only objects are labelled as conditional on the event being active.

## Read alongside the game

Keep [the floor guide](/reference/floors/) nearby, or use search to find a creature, room, item or effect. The guide focuses on visible mechanics; it does not list drop rates, hidden probabilities or internal reward calculations.
